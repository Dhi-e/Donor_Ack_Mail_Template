What We've Been Up To

Our recent work has continued across several learning centres and collaborations:

Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira (RKMV), Belur Math, Howrah

A three-year collaboration agreement has been reached between Dhi-e Foundation and RKMV, Belur — a NAAC A++ accredited college — for joint skill development programmes.

 

Uddipan Educational Trust, Habra, North 24 Parganas

Under a three-year Memorandum of Understanding, we run ongoing English Proficiency and CareerX sessions for underserved rural youth. Uddipan provides the classroom, local volunteers and students, while Dhi-e focuses on curriculum and teaching quality.

 

Dhaspara Bihangam Society, Sagardwip, South 24 Parganas (Sundarbans)

On the remote Sundarbans island of Sagardwip, we partner with Dhaspara Bihangam Society to bring English learning to students who have few such opportunities, with the society hosting classes on the ground.

 

The Social Assistance Institute, Gosaba, South 24 Parganas (Sundarbans)

A collaboration with The Social Assistance Institute supports underprivileged students in the remote village of Sonagaon in Gosaba — a Sundarbans community where many families live under the constant threat of tiger attacks.
